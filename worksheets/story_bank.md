# Behavioural story bank

*From The Data Science Interview Playbook by Mathew Kariuki. Use it with Chapter 13. Aim for six to eight stories that together cover the themes below.*

## Themes to cover

- [ ] A technical success I'm proud of
- [ ] A mistake I made and fixed
- [ ] A disagreement with a stakeholder
- [ ] A vague or ambiguous request
- [ ] A time I influenced a decision with data
- [ ] Prioritising under pressure
- [ ] Learning something new quickly
- [ ] Explaining something technical to a non-technical audience
